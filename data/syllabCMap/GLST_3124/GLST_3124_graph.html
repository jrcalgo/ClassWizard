GLST 3124 - .001: Solidarity Across Borders Section number: 001 Cross-listed with WGST 3050.002 There are no pre- or co-requisites Student learning outcomes: Recognize the political implications of their intersectional positionality in the world, Envision and engage with translation as a political and ethical act of border crossing, Understand different justice and equality struggles as locally and globally interconnected, Learn about various solidarity-based political movements and experiments around the world, Develop a complex understanding of the urgency and possibility of cross-border solidarities.
