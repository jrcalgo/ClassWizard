MGMT 3241 - Acquiring and Maintaining Human Resources; MGMT 3241 Section number: 001 Cross listed courses: none Co-requisites: none Pre-requisites: Management 3140 – grade C Student Learning outcomes: The goal of this class is that students will learn how to 1) explain effective administration of human resources; 2) contribute to organizational effectiveness through HRM; 3) understand basic principles of legal requirements organizations must comply with related to HRM; 5) improve communication and presentation skills. Course Description | Concepts, methods, and issues used in effectively managing human resources in contemporary organizations with a focus on workforce planning, recruitment and selection, organizational entry, socialization, and the legal environment. Experience in developing and utilizing behavioral science research methods to assess effectiveness
