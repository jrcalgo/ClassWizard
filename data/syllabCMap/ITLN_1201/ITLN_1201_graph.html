ITLN 1201 - will cover Chapters 1-3 of Sentieri. This course serves as an introduction to Italian Language and Culture; it will introduce the learners to everyday Italian, and it is designed to provide a variety of tools to develop the four language skills (listening comprehension, speaking, reading, and writing).
