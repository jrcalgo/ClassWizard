BPHD 8650 - Advanced Seminar in Finance 8650 Section number: 002 Cross listed courses: none Co-requisites: none Pre-requisites: none Student Learning outcomes: By the end of the course, students should be able to critically evaluate research in behavioral and experimental finance, identify important open questions, and assess the strengths and limitations of alternative theoretical and empirical approaches. Students will also develop the ability to formulate original research questions and design rigorous studies that can contribute to the academic finance literature. Course Description | This doctoral seminar examines advanced topics in behavioral and experimental finance, with an emphasis on how investor beliefs, preferences, biases, and social interactions affect financial decisions and market outcomes. The course combines theoretical, empirical, and experimental approaches and critically evaluates both foundational and recent research in the literature
