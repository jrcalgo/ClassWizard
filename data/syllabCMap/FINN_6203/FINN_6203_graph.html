FINN 6203 - Financial Economic Theory FINN 6203 Section number: U90 Cross listed courses: ECON 6203 Co-requisites: none Pre-requisites: Admission to the graduate program and permission of the Program Director Student Learning outcomes: This course provides a foundation in asset pricing theory together with an introduction to empirical asset pricing. Students who complete the course will be able to analyze choice under uncertainty, construct no-arbitrage valuations of contingent claims in complete and incomplete markets, apply one-period and multi-period market models, and evaluate the capital asset pricing model (CAPM), arbitrage pricing theory, and the efficient market hypothesis as accounts of observed asset prices. Course Description | A foundation in asset pricing theory with an introduction to empirical asset pricing. Topics include choice under uncertainty, complete and incomplete markets, no-arbitrage valuation of contingent claims, one-period and multi-period market models, the capital asset pricing model (CAPM), arbitrage pricing theory, the efficient market hypothesis, and related topics
