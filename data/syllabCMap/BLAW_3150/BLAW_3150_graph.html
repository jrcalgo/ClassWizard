BLAW 3150 - BLAW 3150 – Business Law I Section number: 093 Instructor: Louis A. Trosch, III Location: Main Campus, Friday 142 1017 Cross listed courses: none Co-requisites: none Pre-requisites: Junior or Senior standing, business major, or permission of department INFO 2130 with a grade of C Student Learning outcomes: The objective of this course is to develop a student's ability to identify, analyze, and resolve problems typically encountered by managers within the framework of legal, regulatory, social and ethical considerations. Students should understand why these considerations are essential to all managers who operate business enterprises. Course Description | A study of the legal setting of business and its relationship to the business firm. Topics include: the nature of law and the court system, criminal and civil procedure, alternative dispute resolution, constitutional authority to regulate business, business ethics, criminal law, torts, contracts, the law of sales, intellectual property, and cyberlaw
