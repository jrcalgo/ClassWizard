SPED 5300 - is designed to introduce the student to the field of special education. Disability areas are addressed as well as the North Carolina standards for general curriculum and adapted curriculum licensure. Current trends and published research in the field of special education are identified and critiqued. In addition, the student will examine a personal philosophy of special education, which reflects the diversity of students with disabilities. (Fall, Spring, Summer)
