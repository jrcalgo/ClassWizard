TRAN 3601 - Introduction to Translation and Interpreting Studies. This 3-hour credit course is taught 100% online, asynchronously. Through a series of theoretical, practical readings, and exercises; students who successfully complete all the tasks should be able to develop the research skills, resources and tools to understand the basics to become a successful translator and interpreter. Students will not be asked to complete translation/interpreting tasks; instead the course focuses on how translators prepare, think, work and accomplish the task of conveying information in another language. Since students come from different backgrounds and have different target languages, class will mostly concentrate on the process instead of the result. Moreover, students are expected to develop their own process to better suit their needs and particular translating and interpreting objectives because "good translators are always in the process of 'becoming' translators—which is to say, learning to translate better, learning more about language and culture and translation" (Robinson).
