FINN 6217 - Blockchain, Cryptocurrencies, and Decentralized Finance FINN 6217 Section number: U90 Cross listed courses: none Co-requisites: none Pre-requisites: FINN 6210 Expertise in a programming language such as C++ or Python Student Learning outcomes: This course is an introduction to blockchain technologies with applications in finance. Students who complete the course will be able to explain how cryptocurrencies are generated and how transactions are validated on blockchains, apply cryptographic primitives including hash functions and public key cryptography, analyze consensus mechanisms such as proof of work and proof of stake, construct smart contracts in Solidity, and evaluate decentralized finance applications, including exchanges, stablecoins, and lending, together with the associated legal and regulatory issues. Course Description | An introduction to blockchain technologies with applications in finance. Blockchain technology enables fast and secure transactions without depending on a trusted party to intermediate. The course studies how cryptocurrencies are generated and how transactions take place on blockchains, the smart contract technology that controls the automated execution of electronic commitments among counterparties, and applications of blockchain technology in financial services, including decentralized finance
