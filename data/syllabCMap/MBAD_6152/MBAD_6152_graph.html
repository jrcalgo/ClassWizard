MBA 6112 - Financial Management 6152 Section number: U90 Cross listed courses: none Co-requisites: none Pre-requisites: MBA 6112 and MBA 6131 with a grade of C Student Learning outcomes: The goal of the course is to learn how a portfolio manager can find out the fundamental values of financial securities and how a corporate manager can evaluate prospective investment projects and make operational and financial decisions to maximize the intrinsic firm value. The valuation process relies on discounted cash flow analysis (DCF), which involves two major components: Cash Flow and Discount Rate. These two parts will be the building blocks of the course. Course Description | Theory and practice of corporate finance including asset management, cost of capital and capital budgeting, optimization problems and socio-economic aspects of financial management.
