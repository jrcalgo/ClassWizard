RSCH 7111 - /8111 Qualitative Research Methods Section 080 Student learning outcomes: Upon completion of the course, the candidate will be able to: Understand major differences between quantitative and qualitative research and identify typical characteristics of qualitative research and naturalistic inquiry process. Identify and explain the range of theories and disciplinary traditions that support major qualitative research designs, and critically analyze the basic assumptions and implications of each qualitative research design/tradition including the interrelationships among reality, knowledge, and value. Reflect on your own presuppositions and subjectivities in regard to the educational research processes. Understand major qualitative research traditions and designs frequently utilized by researchers in education and related fields and understand problems and/or issues pertaining to each research design. Understand the interrelationships among qualitative research purpose/questions, pertinent data sources, data collection methods, and data analysis methods; and understand strategies to assess the results of such efforts. Critically examine various aspects of a qualitative research study published in peer-reviewed journals. Examine various legal/policy issues and ethical dilemmas related to the qualitative research process. Develop relevant strategies, tools and practical skills to collect textual/narrative data. Develop basic analytic strategies, tools, and practical skills for qualitative data analysis Understand the basic structure of qualitative research proposal and/or research report and create a proposal or report for a field-based qualitative research project.
